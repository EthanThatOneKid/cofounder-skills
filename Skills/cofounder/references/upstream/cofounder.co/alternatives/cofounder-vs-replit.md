# Replit + Cofounder: Build your app. Run your business.

> Build in Replit. Use Cofounder MCP to handle email, customer support, CRM, ads, billing, and the rest of your business operations.

## Summary
- [Recommendation](https://cofounder.co/alternatives/cofounder-vs-replit): Build and improve your app in Replit while Cofounder MCP handles the operations around it. Manage customer conversations, find and track prospects, run campaigns, schedule social posts, and manage Stripe billing, with shared company context across the work.

## More business tools in Cofounder MCP
- [Tools for your business operations](https://cofounder.co/alternatives/cofounder-vs-replit#business-tools): Cofounder MCP includes inbox and campaign email, newsletters and transactional app email, CRM-linked customer support, brand research and voice, SEO keyword research, rankings and page audits, website and social analytics, domain registration and DNS, and founder-approved purchases through Link using single-use payment credentials. Prospecting can enrich contact and company details.
