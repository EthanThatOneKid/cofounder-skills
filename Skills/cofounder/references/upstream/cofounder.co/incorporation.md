# Online Company Incorporation with AI

> Incorporate your company online through Ramp inside Cofounder. Use AI-guided setup while you control approvals, attestations, and final review. Start today.

## How online incorporation works
- [Start in Cofounder](https://cofounder.co/incorporation#how-online-incorporation-works): Describe the company you want to build and bring formation into the same workspace as your product, launch, and operating plan.
- [Use Ramp's incorporation flow](https://cofounder.co/incorporation#how-online-incorporation-works): Move through Ramp's company formation experience inside Cofounder instead of assembling a separate stack of disconnected forms and tabs.
- [Review every critical detail](https://cofounder.co/incorporation#how-online-incorporation-works): The workflow pauses when you need to approve information, make an attestation, or complete the final review.
- [Keep building after formation](https://cofounder.co/incorporation#how-online-incorporation-works): Continue running product, engineering, sales, marketing, design, finance, and operations work from Cofounder.
