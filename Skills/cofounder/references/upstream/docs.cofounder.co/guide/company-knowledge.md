# Company Knowledge

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/company-knowledge
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Give your agent what it needs to know about the company by saving it to the Library, and manage Library files.

Put what your agent needs to know about the company in the Library: the
pitch, the market, customer notes, and decisions. Every agent on the company
reads it.
