# Media

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/media
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Make images, SVGs, and short videos and save them to the company Library.

Ask your agent for site art, a logo or icon, campaign creative, or a short
clip for a social post. It makes the file and saves it to the company
Library, where you and your agent can reuse it.
