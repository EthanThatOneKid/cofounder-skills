# Domains

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/domains
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Buy a domain or bring your own, point it at your app, manage its DNS, and send email from it.

Your company gets a domain for its app and its email. Your agent can buy one
or use one you already own, then connect it to your app and your inbox.

Any company member can search names and check a domain's status. Buying,
renewing, importing, connecting, and every DNS read or change need an admin.
