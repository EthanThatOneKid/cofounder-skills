# Incorporate

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/incorporate
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Form your company as a US legal entity through Ramp: connect or apply, pick a state, and submit the paid filing.

Your company becomes a US legal entity, formed through Ramp, with your agent
doing most of the paperwork. Submitting the filing charges Ramp's formation
fee.
