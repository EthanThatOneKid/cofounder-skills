# Slideshows

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/slideshows
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Research viral social slideshows, find background photos, and generate ready-to-post carousels.

Ask your agent to study what performs well on TikTok and Instagram, pick
background photos, and render a finished multi-slide carousel for your product.
Slideshow tools are available on the $50/month plan and above (not available to companies on lower plans). The `niches list`, `library profiles get`, and `list` commands are CLI-only.
