# SEO

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/seo
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Find keywords your site can win, read live Google results and rankings, audit pages, and measure your own Google search traffic.

Pick keywords your site can win, then check where your pages rank after you
ship changes. Your agent looks up what people search for, what Google shows
for a query, which keywords a site ranks for, and how a page scores in an
on-page audit. These lookups cost company credit, usually a few cents or
less. See [Cost and Market](#cost-and-market). Reading your own site's Google
Search Console data is free.
