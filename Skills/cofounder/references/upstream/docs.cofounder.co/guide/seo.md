# SEO

**Description:** Find keywords your site can win, read live Google results and rankings, and audit pages.

Pick keywords your site can win, then check where your pages rank after you
ship changes. Your agent looks up what people search for, what Google shows
for a query, which keywords a site ranks for, and how a page scores in an
on-page audit. Every SEO call costs company credit, usually a few cents or
less. See [Cost and Market](#cost-and-market).
