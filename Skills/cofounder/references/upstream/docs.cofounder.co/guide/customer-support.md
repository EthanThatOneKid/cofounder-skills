# Customer Support

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/guide/customer-support
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Give customers a support inbox. Each email becomes a ticket linked to the sender's CRM contact, and your agent tracks it until it's closed.

Give customers one address to write to, like `support@yourcompany.com`. Each
new email becomes an open ticket linked to the sender's contact in your CRM,
and your agent tracks it until it's closed. Any company member can work
tickets.

This page is about your customers' support. To reach the Cofounder team, use
`support_create` ([Support](/guide/watch-it-run#support)).

Customer support is not available to every company yet. The Support page and
CLI/MCP tools are available only after it is enabled for your company. This is
separate from choosing an inbox: mailbox setup does not enable the feature.
Direct API access and incoming ticket processing remain available.
