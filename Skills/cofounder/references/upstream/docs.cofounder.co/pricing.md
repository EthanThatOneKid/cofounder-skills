# Pricing

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/pricing
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Credits, top-ups, and monthly cost estimates.

Spend usage credit on paid actions like ads, domains, research, and generated
media. Where the new company plans aren't available yet, the $40 monthly
subscription adds $40 in usage credit. Unused credit carries over at renewal
and stays in your balance after the subscription ends.

Where the new company plans are available, prices start at $20 a month.
Monthly credit is spent first. Unused monthly credit expires when a paid
renewal or upgrade starts a new month, or when the plan ends. Top-ups carry
over and stay in your balance after the plan ends.
