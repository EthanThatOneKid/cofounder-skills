# Pricing

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/pricing
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Credits, rollover, and monthly cost estimates.

Plans start at $40 a month, and the whole fee is usage credit. Spend it on paid actions like ads, domains, research, and generated media. Unused credit rolls over monthly, and most businesses never need a top-up.
