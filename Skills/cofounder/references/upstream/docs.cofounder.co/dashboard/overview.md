# Dashboard Overview

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/dashboard/overview
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Answer what your agent needs from you, read the company's mail, contacts, files, and money, and change settings in the CofounderOS dashboard.

Answer what needs you, read the company's mail, contacts, files, and money,
and change settings in the dashboard at
[app.cofounder.co/os](https://app.cofounder.co/os).

Your agent runs the company through MCP or the CLI, and it can do or show
you most of this in chat. A few things need you on the dashboard: adding
payment methods, raising or removing a spending limit or the ad spend
ceiling (the payer only), and entering sign-in credentials. Deleting a
company isn't on MCP either; use the dashboard or `cofounder org delete`.
