# Settings

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/dashboard/settings
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Change your company's members, billing, domains, GitHub, Stripe, secrets, and incorporation, plus your own profile and notifications.

Open the gear on the rail. Company settings apply to everyone in the
company, and some are for admins only. The **You** settings apply only to
you.
