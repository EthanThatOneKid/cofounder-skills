# Home

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/dashboard/home
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Answer what your agent needs from you and see how the company is doing.

Home shows what your agent needs from you and a summary of money, pipeline,
and progress. It's the first screen for each company. The Home tile on the
rail shows a badge with the number of things waiting on you.
