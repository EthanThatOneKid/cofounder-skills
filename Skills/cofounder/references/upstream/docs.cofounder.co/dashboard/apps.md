# Apps

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/dashboard/apps
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Read the company's mail, messages, contacts, money, and files, and pin apps like Roadmap, Domains, and Analytics.

Each app on the rail shows one part of the company. Your agent does the
work, and apps are where you read it, check it, and step in. Most apps also
open inside hosts that display MCP apps, so your agent can show them in
chat.
