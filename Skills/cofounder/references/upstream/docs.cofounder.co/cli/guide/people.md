# People

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/people
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Find hiring candidates or prospects from a plain description, check the evidence for each match, and save the ones you want to the CRM.

Describe who you're looking for, like "founding engineers at seed-stage AI
startups", and your agent finds them. Use it for hiring candidates,
prospects, or anyone else your company wants to reach. Each person comes
back with the evidence for or against your filters, so you judge fit
yourself instead of trusting a score.
