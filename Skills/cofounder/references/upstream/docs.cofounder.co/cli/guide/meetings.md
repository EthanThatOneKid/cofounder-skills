# Meetings

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/meetings
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Book sales calls and follow-ups with your CRM contacts on a teammate's Google Calendar.

Your agent books sales calls and follow-ups with your CRM contacts on your
Google Calendar or a teammate's: find free time → hold the options it
offers → confirm the one the contact picks.
