# CRM

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/crm
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Track the companies, people, and deals you sell to, log what happened with each, and find new prospects.

Every company gets a built-in CRM: accounts (companies), contacts (people),
and opportunities (deals), plus a log of calls, emails, meetings, and notes.
