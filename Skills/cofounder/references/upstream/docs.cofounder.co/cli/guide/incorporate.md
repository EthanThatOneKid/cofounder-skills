# Incorporate

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/incorporate
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Form the company as a US legal entity through Ramp — connect or apply, pick a state, and submit the paid filing.

Cofounder files the company's formation through Ramp. The founder signs in
to Ramp and verifies their identity on Ramp's own pages; your agent handles
the rest of the application and the filing.
