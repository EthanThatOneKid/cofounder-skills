# Domains

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/domains
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Find and buy a domain or bring your own, point it at the company's hosting, and manage its DNS and email sending.

A company's address is a domain it owns. Cofounder can buy one for you or
adopt one you already registered elsewhere, then wire it to the deployed app
and to company email.
