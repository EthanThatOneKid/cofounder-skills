# SEO

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/seo
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Research keywords, read live Google results and rankings, and audit pages.

SEO tools read search data from DataForSEO for one market at a time: what
people search for, what Google shows for a query, which keywords a site ranks
for, and how a page holds up on-page. Use them to choose keywords your site
can win and to check where your pages rank after you ship changes.
