# Create a Company

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/guide/create-a-company
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Create a company, invite the people who share it, and save the settings and business details your agent uses everywhere.

A company holds everything your agent works with: knowledge, connections,
resources, roadmap, and billing. One account can run several companies, each
with its own context.
