# Migrating from the Web App

Source: https://docs.cofounder.co/cli/migrating-from-web-app
Fetched from: https://docs.cofounder.co/llms-full.txt

**Description:** Used Cofounder 2 or Cofounder Canvas? Your account and companies carry over, and your own agent now runs them through MCP or the CLI.

If you used Cofounder 2 or Cofounder Canvas, you keep the same account and
companies. Your companies, files, connections, and billing carry over
unchanged. What changes is who does the work: your own agent, through MCP or
the `cofounder` CLI. To check on things yourself, use the
[dashboard](/dashboard/overview) at app.cofounder.co/os.
